# Site map

The site is one world with five places. Islands A–D are placeholder names: rename freely, and keep the channel numbers and accent tokens.

| Place | Channel | Accent | Built like (reference) | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Entrance + Gate** | CH-00 | `signal` | rikkazzz.com: entrance, line design, visitor guidance | Line-drawn intro (line → frame → title stroke → travel guide), then VISITOR ACCESS beside a 3:4 character key-visual slot. Type a callsign or ENTER AS GUEST, and the callsign greets the visitor in the HUD. |
| **Hub** | — | all four | rikkazzz.com: line design | A night sky with four line-art islands joined by route lines. **Move the mouse (or scroll) to travel**: the camera follows the pointer and settles on the nearest island. Click the focused island to dive in. |
| **Island A — Works** | CH-01 | `island-a` | ryuse1-studio.com | A featured work fills the screen. Below it is a numbered horizontal strip of works (No.01…), and each work opens as a full-bleed panel page. |
| **Island B — About** | CH-02 | `island-b` | steffieharner.netlify.app | A short, big-type self-intro with a profile panel, likes (ZZZ, manga), tools and a timeline. Paper theme toggle for the reading view. |
| **Island C — Story / Lab** | CH-03 | `island-c` | cyberfiction-five.vercel.app | A scroll-scrubbed cinematic: a canvas image sequence of the city/islands told in manga captions. The playground for experiments and process stories. |
| **Island D — Board** | CH-04 | `island-d` | (own) | Contact, socials and a small guestbook styled as a street notice board, with stickers for links. |

**Navigation rules.** Travel between islands is always mouse- or scroll-driven on the hub, never a click. A click only *enters* the island the visitor has arrived at. Every island page carries the HUD with a "◀ HUB" tab that returns to the hub with the camera on that island. Islands never link to each other with a plain page load. Deep links (/works/no-07) land directly with a short arrival wipe, and the HUD lets the visitor jump back.
