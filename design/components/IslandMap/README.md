The hub: a night-sky world travelled by **moving the mouse**, drawn in the line language (route lines, orbit rings, crosshair, coordinate readout, a dashed tether to the focused island).

Pointer position across the viewport maps onto the camera's travel range, and the wheel or trackpad nudges the same value, so horizontal and vertical motion move the camera. Islands near the target pull the camera in (a magnet, so the visitor settles on an island rather than between them). The island nearest the camera is **focused**: its top fills with its accent, its leader line draws and its card shows. Clicking a focused island **enters** it with a speed-line dive (650ms), then `onEnter` fires. Clicking an unfocused island only travels to it. Keyboard: Tab focuses each island button and moves the camera there, and Enter on the focused island enters it.

Provide `islands` (with `x`/`y` in world pixels inside `worldWidth` × `worldHeight`, default 2400 × 1500, plus an optional `blurb`), `routes` (pairs of ids, default a loop), `onEnter`, an optional `onFocus`, an optional `foreground` (decorative nodes on a 1.25× parallax layer) and `hint={false}` to skip the travel hint. The container needs a height. Tuning lives in `IslandMap.timing` (`ease`, `snapRadius`, `focusRadius`, `wheel`, `dive`). With reduced motion, travel has no easing and the dive is a 200ms fade.
